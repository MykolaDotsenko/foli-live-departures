Turku Departures is an independent, privacy-first transit companion for Turku and Åbo.

SEE WHAT LEAVES NEXT
• Live and scheduled bus departures with clear realtime, planned, stale and unknown states.
• Relevant disruption notices next to the journey decision they affect.
• Favourite stops, recent stops and local saved places built from public stop identities.

KNOW WHEN TO PRESS STOP
Ride Mode follows the exact trip stop order and can combine Föli realtime evidence with optional on-device location. It gives conservative prompts such as “get ready” and “get off now” only when the available evidence supports them.

On Android, you can separately opt in for the active ride to use a foreground companion. It keeps a visible system notification while that ride is active. The app does not request background-location permission, and device-dependent locked-phone behaviour is not guaranteed.

RECOVER WHEN THINGS GO WRONG
Useful local state, recent same-stop departures, backup stops, driver cards and route hand-offs remain available when connectivity or the normal trip flow degrades.

PRIVACY BY DEFAULT
• No account.
• No advertising SDK.
• No analytics SDK.
• Saved places use public stop numbers/names rather than street addresses.
• Optional device location is processed on the device and is not sent by Turku Departures to the developer or to Föli.
• Direct address/place lookup is disabled in the production app.

Turku Departures uses Föli open data and is not made by or affiliated with Föli or the City of Turku. For tickets and official journey planning, use Föli's own services.
